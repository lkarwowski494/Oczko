C · Counter drum

Idea: the clicker from your project bag, rebuilt for the screen: a number drum that turns one notch per row and shows what comes next.

Signature element: the drum = previous, current and next row; a notched tab on the next row when the pattern asks for something; a second drum for the repeat; the lock works like the lever on a mini counter.
